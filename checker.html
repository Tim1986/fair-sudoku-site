Verdict

The checker runs the same human-technique engine that certifies Fair Sudoku’s own puzzles: naked/hidden singles, naked/hidden pairs, pointing pairs, and box–line reduction. A puzzle that defeats the full toolkit requires chain logic or bifurcation — the “long goofy counterfactuals” Fair Sudoku exists to abolish.
